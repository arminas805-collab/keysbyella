# Instagram Reels algorithm and posting timing (as of 2026-11-23), small faceless piano-education account (~19 followers, US + Western Europe)

Evidence quality note: official Instagram/Mosseri statements are labelled "OFFICIAL". Third-party studies are labelled by source and are correlational. Several vendor blogs (SocialPilot, dev.to, etc.) mix official facts with uncited claims; those are flagged as unofficial.

## 1. Which ranking signals matter most for Reels reach to non-followers; what Mosseri / Instagram officially said (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
Instagram's officially named top three ranking signals are watch time, likes (per reach) and sends (per reach). Likes matter slightly more for connected (follower) content and sends slightly more for unconnected (non-follower) content. Saves, completion rate and "early-minutes velocity" are not officially ranked by Instagram; they are third-party inferences.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL (Mosseri video series, reported Jan 22, 2025): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He told creators to review "average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach" in Insights. "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." — [Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- OFFICIAL (Mosseri Reel, reported July 2024): "One of the most important signals we use in ranking is sends per reach"; he added "don't force it as a creator." The article is dated July 3, 2024, so this is older than 2025. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389)
- OFFICIAL (Mosseri, Feb 25, 2025): Instagram looks at both the percentage of a video watched and the raw seconds watched, so longer videos are not penalised. His example: 10 seconds watched on a 1-minute video counts the same as 10 seconds on a 10-second video. Weighting between the two was not disclosed. — [Social Media Today, Feb 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-longer-video-watch-time-versus-completion-rate/740916/)
- OFFICIAL (Instagram creators page): Each eligible reel first goes to a small audience, whether or not they follow you. Top performers then reach wider audiences, so engagement from non-followers drives breakthrough. — [Instagram Creators: Recommendations and originality](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- OFFICIAL (Mosseri blog, May 31, 2023; older): Reels ranking looks at the viewer's activity (likes, saves, reshares, comments), history with the poster, information about the reel (audio and visuals, popularity) and information about the poster. Reels lean more on entertainment and how likely a viewer is to find a reel engaging. — [Social Media Today, May 2023](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-explainer-feed-reels-and-stories-algorithm/651705/)
- OFFICIAL (Mosseri, interview reported Apr 8, 2025): "Hashtags are no longer a primary way to increase your reach on Instagram." He said small ranking changes happen daily and major ones are rare; sudden reach drops are more likely external (holidays, shifts in when audiences are online). — [Social Media Today, Apr 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)
- OFFICIAL (Instagram, Aug 2025): Reels Insights now include a Retention chart per reel ("the flatter the line, the more engaged your audience is"). "Skip rate" replaces "view rate" and is "the percentage of views from people who decided to skip your reel during those first 3 seconds." — [Social Media Today, Aug 24, 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/)
- OFFICIAL timeline (via a vendor summary; treat dates as secondary): Feb 2025, reels up to 3 minutes eligible for Explore and non-follower recommendation; Dec 2025, maximum creation length rose to 20 minutes (the article says the non-follower recommendation ceiling stays 3 minutes); "Your Algorithm" interest controls launched Dec 2025 (US), expanded Jan 2026 and later in 2026. The same source reports no confirmed ranking changes in Aug-Sep 2026. — [SocialPilot, Sept 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm)
- UNOFFICIAL: the SocialPilot article claims DM sends count "3-5x more than likes" for non-follower reach. It is not in the Mosseri statements quoted anywhere above, so it should not be presented as fact. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm)

### Inferences
- For a 19-follower account nearly all reach is "unconnected", so sends per reach and average watch time are the two metrics to optimise. Likes per reach is the third.
- Saves are not in Mosseri's top three; treat saves as a useful diagnostic for educational content (a likely proxy for "send-worthy"), not as a confirmed ranking lever.
- "Early velocity" in the first minutes/hours is not documented by Instagram. The only official statement is that a reel is first tested on a small audience and widened if it performs. A first-hour check is therefore a reasonable proxy, not a rule.

### Gaps
- No official weighting between watch time, likes and sends, or between percent watched and seconds watched.
- No official statement found on saves, loops/replays or comments as ranking signals in 2025-2026.
- No official Instagram statement found that early-minutes velocity matters.

## 2. Penalties: watermarks, resolution, reposts/duplicates, AI labels, hashtag limits; hashtag and keyword/SEO guidance

### Takeaway
Confirmed: visible watermarks make content ineligible for recommendations, duplicate or unoriginal reposts are demoted, and Instagram now caps hashtags at 5 per caption. AI "labels" only matter for profiles that feature AI-generated people. A human-played piano account with AI-assisted editing is not affected.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL: "Eligible content must have no visible watermarks." If identical copies exist, only the original is recommended and replaces copies in recommendation surfaces (when the original is relatively new and matched by audio/visual signals). Significantly changed content (meme edit, new voiceover, reaction remix) is not replaced. Accounts that repeatedly repost without creating or materially enhancing become ineligible for recommendations, can check Account Status and appeal. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- OFFICIAL: unoriginal content largely repurposed without added value, clickbait and engagement bait may not be recommended. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- OFFICIAL (Apr 30, 2026): originality protections that already applied to Reels extended to photos and carousels. Accounts that mostly repost are no longer eligible for recommendations. Watermark or speed-change edits do not count as material edits. Followers still see the content. — [TechCrunch, Apr 30, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- Aggregator test is on a rolling 30-day basis (most of an account's posts being reposts). Penalty duration was not stated in this source. A vendor summary says penalties last 24 hours to 30 days and cut reach to non-followers only; I could not verify that against an official source. — [Social Media Today, Apr 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-originality-reach-penalties/819016/); [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm)
- OFFICIAL (May 2023; older): watermarked or low-resolution, muted, bordered and mostly-text Reels get less reach. — [Social Media Today, May 2023](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-explainer-feed-reels-and-stories-algorithm/651705/)
- OFFICIAL (reported Dec 18, 2025): Instagram will "gradually update" the hashtag cap per reel or post caption to 5 (earlier tests capped many users at 3). Instagram says "fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones" improve performance. Mosseri has said hashtags do not boost reach but help show what a post is about. — [Social Media Today, Dec 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- OFFICIAL (Aug 31, 2026): Instagram renamed "AI creator" label to "AI-generated profile". Profiles featuring AI-generated people without the label may have reach reduced. Using AI to edit photos, polish captions or create graphics does not require a label. Effective date for reach limits not stated. — [TechCrunch, Aug 31, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/)
- Keywords: vendor advice is to replace hashtag blocks with keyword-rich captions matching topics people search (unofficial). — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm)

### Inferences
- For a faceless piano account: never reuse clips with TikTok/CapCut watermarks; export clean; avoid posting the same clip twice from another account; do not re-post other pianists' content without substantial transformation.
- Use 3-5 specific hashtags at most (e.g. niche piano-learning topics), and put the plain-language topic (e.g. "left hand pattern for Clair de Lune") in the first caption line, on-screen text and spoken/visible text. Hashtags are for categorisation, not reach.
- If the faceless account never shows an AI-generated human, the AI-profile label does not apply.

### Gaps
- No official statement found on a specific low-resolution threshold in 2025-2026 (only the 2023 mention).
- No official penalty duration for aggregator status.
- No official statement found on how much captions/keywords weigh in Reels recommendation (as opposed to search).

## 3. Optimal Reel length, first-3-seconds guidance, trial reels

### Takeaway
Instagram's own metrics (skip rate = first 3 seconds, Retention chart) tell you the opening is the key measured moment. Length data shows only modest effects: Buffer's 2026 data favours 90-179 seconds for reach, but this is an association vs each account's own baseline, not a rule for tiny accounts. Trial Reels let you test with non-followers without exposing them to the follower base.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer, 2.73M Reels (published Jan 1 - Aug 15, 2026; article Nov 12, 2026): reach vs account baseline: 90-179s +7.4%; 30-59s +2.7%; 60-89s +1.6%; 15-29s -0.9%; under 15s -1.5%; 180s+ -49.3%. Buffer calls the effect "real but modest"; median account saw nearer +4%. Over-3-minute reels had the highest engagement rate but about half the reach. No small-account breakdown. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length)
- Skip rate and Retention definitions: see section 1 (Aug 2025). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/)
- Trial Reels: launched Dec 10, 2024. Shown to non-followers first; followers do not see them in Feed or Reels tab (some may see via DMs or audio/location pages). Not on the profile grid until shared. Auto-share option judges performance on views in the first 72 hours. Insights appear about 24 hours after sharing and compare with your previous trials. — [Instagram Creators / Meta](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- Eligibility: a vendor source says Trial Reels opened to public accounts with 1,000+ followers in July 2025 and quotes Mosseri: "a great way to test if your content ranks highly." The official page fetched does not list the follower minimum. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm); [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- Hook guidance "motion or text within ~3 seconds, ideally within 500 ms" and "90% niche consistency" are unofficial vendor claims. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm)

### Inferences
- Start with the most striking audible/visible moment (the finished sound, or a clear on-screen promise) in the first second; compare skip rate across reels.
- With ~19 followers, Trial Reels may not be available or may be unnecessary (all reach is non-follower anyway). Check the toggle in the app; if absent, rely on normal posting.
- For education reels, 30-90 seconds is a defensible range; do not go under ~15s unless the content is one tight idea. Buffer's effects are small relative to hook and content quality.

### Gaps
- No official ideal length. No data specific to piano/education niche or to accounts under 100 followers.
- Trial Reels follower threshold unverified from an official source.

## 4. Best posting hours and days (US ET/PT, Western Europe); frequency; does 3x/day hurt

### Takeaway
Independent studies disagree, which indicates there is no universal best hour. Evenings (6-9 pm local) and weekday mornings/lunch (9 am-12 pm) recur. Mosseri says there is no direct link between posting activity and reach, and that timing shifts usually matter less than content. Buffer shows more posts per week modestly raises per-post reach with diminishing returns; no major source tested 3 posts per day.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer, 9.6M posts (Jan 2024-Dec 2025; article Sept 23, 2026; all formats; median engagement rate; local time): best Thursday 9 am, Wednesday 12 pm, Wednesday 6 pm. Best days Wed, Thu, Tue; weakest Fri and Sat. Evenings 6-11 pm perform best every weekday except Thursday. Suggested 3-5 feed posts per week. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/best-time-to-post-on-instagram)
- Metricool 2026 study, 24.4M posts from 375k accounts (June 2026; all formats; time zone not stated): window 6-9 pm, peak 8-9 pm; lowest activity 2-5 am. Its Reels-specific table is from a 2024 study (not 2026): highest average views at 12 am; Sunday best day; the most common upload time was 6 pm but with low average views. Table and text disagree for Thu-Sun. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-instagram/)
- HubSpot 2026 Instagram Engagement Report (survey of 601 marketers in US, UK, India, NZ; updated Aug 25, 2026; local time): Reels best weekdays 9 am-12 pm, secondary 6-9 pm; avoid 6-9 am. North America: 9 am-12 pm Mon-Wed, 12-3 pm Fri/Sat. UK/Ireland: 9 am-12 pm weekdays, Saturday 12-3 pm and 6-9 pm. No continental-Europe data. This is a marketer survey, not platform behavior data. — [HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/instagram-best-time-post)
- Adobe Express study of 22,125 Reels from 200 top creators (reported Jun 2025; time zone and region not stated): highest average views Tuesday, noon-2 pm; 3 am also strong; Saturday and midnight highest likes; 2 am worst for views. Authors say no single best time applies across categories. Large-creator sample, limited relevance. — [PetaPixel, Jun 20, 2025](https://petapixel.com/2025/06/20/these-are-the-best-times-and-days-to-post-reels-on-instagram)
- Buffer, 2.1M feed posts from ~102k accounts (published Aug 13, 2025; Reels, photos, carousels pooled; Stories excluded): per-post reach vs 1-2 posts/week: about +12% at 3-5 posts, +18% at 6-9, +24% at 10+; "diminishing returns". Multiple posts per day not analysed. No small-account breakdown. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
- Mosseri (interview reported Apr 8, 2025): no direct correlation in the system between posting activity and reach; sudden reach drops more likely external. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)
- Vendor guidance of 3-4 high-impact Reels per week is unofficial. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm)
- Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Later, Socialinsider data: search surfaced Sprout (2024 version only) and Later/Hootsuite pages, but I did not retrieve a 2025-2026 dataset from them; Socialinsider's 2025 benchmark PDF appeared in results but was not read.

### Inferences
- Candidate starting slots, to be tested (not proven): US audience: 12 pm ET (9 am PT) weekday and 6-8 pm ET (3-5 pm PT) are the overlap of Buffer/HubSpot/Metricool; Western Europe: 12-1 pm CET and 6-9 pm CET (local evening). Because ET 12 pm = 6 pm CET, one post around 12-1 pm ET reaches Europe in the evening and the US at lunch; a second at ~6-7 pm ET reaches the US evening and Europe at night (midnight CET), which Metricool's 2024 Reels data suggests is not necessarily bad.
- Since a 19-follower account relies on recommendation and not follower activity, posting hour likely matters less than for large accounts; this is an inference consistent with Mosseri's "no direct correlation" statement.
- No evidence found that posting 2-3 times per day is penalised; also no evidence it helps beyond diminishing returns. Safest approach: 1/day (or up to 2/day with different ideas, spaced at least several hours apart), prioritise quality; do not post near-duplicates (originality/duplicate rules).

### Gaps
- No study found that isolates accounts under 100 followers, faceless accounts or the piano/music niche.
- No source found that tests multiple posts per day versus one; claim "3x/day hurts" is unsupported either way.
- No Western-Europe-specific 2025-2026 dataset retrieved from a major vendor; a small site (timetopost.co) appeared for France/Germany but was not read.
- Sprout Social 2025-2026 and Hootsuite 2025-2026 times not retrieved.

## 5. Daily measurement process and decision rules

### Takeaway
Use the metrics Instagram itself names (average watch time, sends per reach, likes per reach) plus the new Retention chart and skip rate. Thresholds below are working heuristics, not published benchmarks.

### Cited Findings
- Metrics to check, per Mosseri: average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. — [Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- Skip rate (first 3 seconds) and Retention chart are in Reels Insights since Aug 2025. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/)
- Trial Reels give Insights about 24 hours after sharing and compare to previous trials. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- Instagram's own testing model: small initial audience, then wider if it performs. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)

### Inferences
- Record per reel: views at ~1 h, 24 h and 72 h; reach; skip rate; average watch time and % watched; sends/reach; saves/reach; likes/reach; follows gained; format tag; hook tag; posting slot.
- Rank reels by sends/reach and average watch time first. Repeat or iterate a format when it is in your own top quartile on sends/reach and watch time after 3+ posts, and skip rate is below your own median. Drop or rework when two consecutive posts of that format sit in your bottom quartile.
- Compare only against your own baseline (as Buffer does) because no published benchmark exists for accounts this small.
- Treat timing tests as slow: change one variable (posting slot) at a time, repeat each slot at least 3-4 times before concluding, since single-post variance will exceed timing effects.
- Early-velocity (1 h) numbers are a leading indicator only; Instagram has not confirmed it is a ranking input.

### Gaps
- No published thresholds for "good" sends/reach or watch time. The thresholds above are not sourced.
- No official guidance on how many repeats of a format justify scaling.
