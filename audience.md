# Keys by Ella: target audience and posting times (2026-11-23)

## Who we target
- US first, then UK, Canada, Australia and Western Europe (FR, DE, NL, Nordics). English captions, no spoken language needed.
- Age 13-34: Gen Z and Gen Alpha beginners, plus millennials who "always wanted to learn piano".
- Interests: easy piano tutorials of famous songs, satisfying 3D/animated music, Christmas songs (Nov-Dec), movie/game/pop melodies, classical hits (Für Elise, Moonlight).
- Behaviour: watch with sound on, save tutorials to practise later (saves matter most), comment keywords for free sheet music.
- Our own account has 19 followers, so Instagram shows no audience demographics yet (needs 100+). Revisit when it unlocks.

## Peak hours (sources: Sprout Social, PostFast 5,191-Reel study, all in audience local time)
- Weekday afternoon is strongest: about 12:00-14:00 US Eastern (17:00-19:00 UTC). Tue-Wed best.
- European morning is less competitive for small accounts (Tue 10:00 UTC: 1.43x its account average).
- Saturday midday also over-performs on a relative basis (Sat 14:00 UTC).
- Dead zone: 1-5 AM local time.
- Caveat: these studies show when posts were published, not where viewers live. We verify with our own data after 2 weeks.

- A = Europe late morning/lunch. B = US lunch + Europe evening (best overlap). C = US prime evening.
- Rule: first reel of the day in slot B gets the strongest content (tutorial with hook); C gets satisfying/3D.

## Review
- Weekly analysis compares views by slot. After 2 weeks drop the weakest slot or shift it by 1-2 hours.
